--- TITLE ---
Esau Was Told He Would Serve His Brother. He Never Did.

--- CAPTION / DESCRIPTION ---
Esau was told he would serve his brother. He never did — not once in his lifetime.

Read it for yourself:

"By your sword you shall live, And you shall serve your brother; And it shall come to pass, when you become restless, That you shall break his yoke from your neck." — Genesis 27:40

That was Isaac's word over Esau. And yet the record of Scripture is that Esau never served Jacob a single day of his life.

So what happened? Esau refused to play to that decree. In the moment he heard it, he understood something many believers never come to: he had an option. To serve, or not to serve. And he took responsibility for his own life.

Here is the lesson: the power may not necessarily be in what was said to you, but in how you have treated what was said to you.

If you allow the wrong mindset, you will begin to live a life of defeat. But you can choose to live against the narrative — and walk in victory.

— Pst Emmanuel Omini, The NewWine Place
