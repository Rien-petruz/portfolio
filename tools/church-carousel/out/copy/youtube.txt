--- TITLE ---
He Was Told He Would Serve — He Never Did | Genesis 27:40

--- CAPTION / DESCRIPTION ---
Isaac told Esau he would serve his brother — but Esau never served Jacob a single day of his life. What does that tell you?

"By your sword you shall live, And you shall serve your brother; And it shall come to pass, when you become restless, That you shall break his yoke from your neck." — Genesis 27:40

Esau refused to play to that decree. He chose his reality. At that moment he knew he had an option — to serve, or not to serve — and he took responsibility for his own life.

The power may not necessarily be in what was said to you, but in how you have treated what was said to you.

If you allow the wrong mindset, you'll begin to live a life of defeat. But you can choose to live against the narrative and walk in victory.
