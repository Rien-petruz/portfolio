--- TITLE ---
Choose A People, And You Have Chosen A God | Naomi, Ruth & Orpah

--- DESCRIPTION / CAPTION ---
When Naomi lost her husband, and later her two sons, she offered a way out for her two daughters in law.

She told them to go back to their PEOPLE, and their God. The statement simply means they should go back to their people and their people's god.

This means that anytime you choose to associate with a people, you will eventually be identified with their god.

That's why when Ruth was going to declare her loyalty, she said "your people will be my people, and your God my God..."

So, a Christian is not just one who identifies with Christians but one who truly serves their God. In other words, what makes you a Christian is that you have declared Jesus to be your God.

If truly we (believers) are your people, then our Jesus must be your Jesus also. You can't claim to be one of us but fight everything about Jesus in your public space.

We know our people because they love our Jesus as their God.

— Pst Emmanuel Omini
