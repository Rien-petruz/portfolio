--- TITLE ---
Whatever You Call It Is What It Will Answer To

--- DESCRIPTION / CAPTION ---
Like Adam, sometimes you have the opportunity to decide what to call the things you see around you. And whatever you decide to call them is what they will answer to.

If you have strange dreams, change the whole narrative. Don't allow them decide what they want to be to you.

You have authority over your own realities. Decide them.

Whatever you do not like, change the narrative. Don't just sit there and worry when you should be "waring" with your words.

You have the power!

— Pst Emmanuel Omini
