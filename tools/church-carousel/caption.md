# Post caption — Esau & the decree

**Slides:** `out/slide-01.png` → `out/slide-08.png` (post in order, 1080×1350)

---

He was told he would serve. He never did.

*"By your sword you shall live, And you shall serve your brother; And it shall come to pass, when you become restless, That you shall break his yoke from your neck."* — Genesis 27:40

Isaac told Esau that he shall "serve" his brother.

Guess what? Esau never served Jacob in his lifetime.

What does that tell you? Esau refused to play to that decree. He chose his reality.

At that moment, he knew he had an option to serve or not to serve. He took responsibility for his own life.

The power may not necessarily be in what was said to you, but in how you have treated what was said to you.

If you allow the wrong mindset, you'll begin to live a life of defeat.

But you can choose to live against the narrative and walk in victory.
